- Stopping the service no longer reports recovery debt solely because the initial restoration time budget ran out. Deferred changes stay recorded for the follow-up restore.
- Persistent restore failures no longer re-announce recovery debt on every screen cycle; new failures and access/debt transitions still notify, and every failed restore remains in the journal.
- Teardown cleanup survives follow-up scheduling failures and the timed restore wakelock's release race.

Caveat: Android 14+ may freeze a cached process during follow-up restoration. This remains a physical-device verification seam; the durable ledger retains unfinished work for later recovery.
