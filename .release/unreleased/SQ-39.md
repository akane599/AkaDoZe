# SQ-39

- Start the teardown command budget on the worker and queue one deadline-free, restore-only follow-up for incomplete or failed teardown, protected by a separate 30-second partial wakelock.
- Notify once per retained ledger entry on each restore pass, including timeouts, unknown readbacks, access loss and failed durable cleanup.
- Mark unverified Doze and motion-sensor readbacks honestly, and keep concurrent non-test journal events in their real session.
- Share the SHELL-or-ROOT session rule with self-tests; preserve APP-level capabilities for recovery.
